Echo

Everyone deserves to be heard. Echo makes that real.

Echo is an AI-powered speech infrastructure platform that enables people with non-standard speech to communicate more naturally. It creates personalised speech recognition models that learn an individual’s unique speech patterns, while also supporting underrepresented languages and speech varieties.

The engine

The voices were never the problem. AI just hadn’t learned them.

Every person speaks differently, even with the same speech condition. So Echo doesn’t ask one model to understand everyone. Each person contributes samples of their own speech, and Echo builds a personalised adapter on top of a powerful base model such as Whisper.

Step 1

Record in your own voice.

A guided recording tool walks you through phrases at your own pace. Switch between Simple, Medium and Advanced levels, or add your own phrases, then record and save them.

Step 2

Echo builds a model that knows you.

Once you’ve recorded, Echo automatically builds your personalised model, ready to use. Your voice journey shows each level you complete, the phrases you’ve recorded, and the languages Echo has learned.

Live captions

Speak naturally. Everyone follows along.

Live captioning is one of Echo’s core applications: real-time captions of your speech across phone calls, video calls, live presentations, meetings and face-to-face conversations.

One link. Everyone’s in the conversation.

Echo turns your personalised speech into text in real time, so the people you’re talking with can follow without you repeating yourself or relying on someone else to interpret. Start a session, copy the link and send it to anyone. You can pause captions whenever you need, and viewers see the same captions and transcript as you speak.

Nothing to install

Anyone with the link can join from their browser.

Works with any platform

Echo isn’t tied to one tool. Use it alongside any call, stream or conversation.

Powered by your voice

Captions come from the model you trained, so they follow the way you actually speak.

Dictation

Say it in your own voice. Echo types it for you.

For people who find typing hard, and aren’t understood by ordinary voice tools, the model you trained becomes a way to write. Dictate messages, emails, documents and notes with Echo, a Chrome extension that adapts to you and works in the tools you already use.

Step 1

Pick a language and press start.

Open Echo from your toolbar, choose English or Swahili, and start with one click or your own keyboard shortcut.

Step 2

Speak. Echo does the rest.

A small indicator shows that Echo is listening. When you stop, your personalised model processes what you said.

Step 3

Your words land where you are.

The transcript is formatted and placed straight into the field you’re in, whether that’s a message to an AI assistant, an email or a search box. No copying and pasting.

AAC

Echo also powers AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) applications, enabling people with speech disabilities to communicate through personalised text and voice.

Accessibility infrastructure

Together, these applications extend Echo beyond a traditional speech-to-text system into an accessibility infrastructure that can support communication across everyday, professional, educational, and digital environments.
